# Art direction previews

These are comparison samples, not screenshots of the playable game or completed animation assets.

- `procedural-sample.svg` and `procedural-sample.png`: editable code-drawn shapes, rendered with ImageMagick. The player is shown at 70 px and furnace at 104 px in the scale strip.
- `illustrated-sample.png`: generated with the built-in image generation tool. The scale strip is illustrative; its pixel-height labels are conceptual rather than a measured production sprite contract.

## Illustrated sample prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: preview art-direction board for a playable isometric woodland survival/crafting videogame; not a finished game screenshot. Create a beautifully polished ILLUSTRATED 2D SPRITE sample, landscape board around 1000x750. Warm cream background. Heading exactly '2 · ILLUSTRATED SPRITES'. Two large separated subjects side by side with ample margins: left a friendly adult woodland settler player character, three-quarter isometric view looking down 30 degrees, teal/green simple tunic, brown trousers and leather boots, brown hair, small basic iron axe with wood handle held at side. Adult compact proportions, not chibi, no armor, no steampunk, no sci-fi. Right a primitive basic stone furnace, waist/chest high relative to player, rounded hand-stacked warm grey stones with masonry arch glowing orange with embers, short stone chimney, tiny thin smoke plume, few logs beside it; no bellows or metal machines (this is a BASIC furnace). Both stand on small mossy grass diamond ground patches with soft contact shadows, same camera and lighting. Labels beneath exactly 'Player' and 'Basic furnace'. Bottom strip labelled exactly 'Game scale' shows the same two subjects reduced to plausible game sizes: player around 65px tall and furnace around 100px tall, to judge readability. Style: smooth hand-painted storybook game assets, clean readable silhouettes, subtly textured stone, cloth folds and warm natural lighting. Painterly material detail while clear at small scale, no pixel art, no outlines as thick as cartoons, no huge scenic backdrop, no UI beyond the requested labels. This board demonstrates an attainable illustrated sprite direction for an indie survival game inspired by Terraria's natural charm but not its pixel style and not Factorio's industrial visual style. Do not add extra characters or props.
